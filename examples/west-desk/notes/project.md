# west-desk

Current overview of the book and its archive. Kept under 2,000 words; older
entries live in `notes/history/` with their citations. Unfinished work is in
`notes/recovery/summer-2001-fan-out.md`.

## The book

An invented memoir of the west power desk at Meridian, Houston, April 2001
to early 2002, told by Ruth Kessler. Three chapters exist. The archive is
the desk's mail, memos and journal entries under `sources/`.

## Chapters written

- `manuscript/ch01-the-desk.md # The room` to `# The checklist`: covers
  2001-04; Ruth joins the desk, the cast, the noon cutoff, the checklist.
  People active: Marcus Idowu, Priya Venkataraman, Tomas Lindqvist, Corinne
  Baptiste, Wes Hargrove. Threads opened: the checklist, the Cascade tie
  rating. Themes: the desk as apprenticeship.
- `manuscript/ch02-the-cutoff.md # The eleven o'clock rule` to `# What June
  was going to be`: covers 2001-05-16 to 2001-06-04. The cutoff moves from
  noon to eleven (SRC-000005 p1, SRC-000006 p1); the congestion working group
  on 2001-05-30 (SRC-000001 p1); the Portland call and the moved Friday
  schedule (SRC-000002 p1). Threads opened: the working group, the summer
  flow south. Threads closed: the noon cutoff. The chapter has two sections
  headed "Morning"; cite them by quoting the sentence that follows, never by
  number.
- `manuscript/ch03-the-outage.md # Ten past eleven` to `# What the statement
  said`: covers 2001-06-19 to 2001-06-21, looking ahead to 2001-11. The
  Cascade tie forced outage at 23:10 on 2001-06-19 (SRC-000003 p3), the
  stepped derate, forty then sixty (SRC-000004 p2), Tomas holding the noon
  submission (SRC-000012 p2), the letter on a 1998 template (SRC-000022),
  Corinne's October settlement dispute raised 2001-11-08 (SRC-000008 p1).
  Threads opened: the settlement dispute. Threads continuing: the summer
  flow.
